- PubMed / NCBI literature search
- ClinicalTrials.gov API integration
- structured paper records
- trial status tracking
- duplicate-paper detection
- study-type classification
- DOI / PMID / NCT linking
- evidence tables
- source freshness timestamps

## V3 — Evidence graph

Connect:

- hallmarks
- pathways
- genes
- proteins
- interventions
- biomarkers
- diseases / age-related conditions
- papers
- clinical trials

The graph should preserve evidence direction and study type rather than implying that association equals causation.

## V4 — Public-data research

Candidate capabilities:

- reproducible notebooks
- dataset provenance
- cohort filtering
- survival analysis
- longitudinal biomarker analysis
- visualization
- model cards for any machine-learning work
- exportable research reports

Public datasets should be used according to their licenses, data-use agreements, and privacy requirements.

## V5 — Local research assistant

A local AI research assistant can:

- summarize imported papers
- compare studies
- flag contradictions
- identify missing evidence
- generate structured research notes
- trace every factual claim back to a source

The assistant should not silently turn weak evidence into medical recommendations.
