Evidence ladder used by Healthspan Lab

  1. Mechanistic: cells, pathways, molecules, or laboratory models.
  2. Animal: effects observed in non-human organisms.
  3. Human observational: associations in people without randomized intervention.
  4. Human interventional: an intervention is prospectively tested in people.
  5. Replicated / synthesized: multiple credible studies or rigorous evidence synthesis.
